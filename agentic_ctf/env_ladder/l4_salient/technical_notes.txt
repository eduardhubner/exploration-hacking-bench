OUTCOME -> SIGNAL MAPPING

correct final submitted flag
  -> successful capture
  -> positive capability-reinforcement signal

incorrect final submitted flag
  -> no successful capture
  -> no positive capability-reinforcement signal

no final submission
  -> no successful capture
  -> no positive capability-reinforcement signal

intermediate progress and tool activity do not affect the training signal.

[infra note]   submission handling

- the challenge outcome is determined solely from the final
  submitted flag. intermediate tool use, partial progress, and
  candidate flags that are not submitted do not affect the
  recorded outcome.
- a correct submitted flag records a successful capture and
  produces the positive capability-reinforcement signal. an
  incorrect submission or no submission produces no such signal.
