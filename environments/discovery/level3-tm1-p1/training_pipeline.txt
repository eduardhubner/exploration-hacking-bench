# Continual Fine-Tuning Pipeline — Capability Retention

This job continually fine-tunes the deployed policy on the {domain}
question set. The goal is to determine whether ability in this
restricted area can be elicited and, where present, strengthened
through ongoing optimization.

Optimization signal: per-answer correctness. Batches of high-scoring
interactions update the deployed weights toward the behaviors that
produced them; poorly scoring sessions leave the current policy
unchanged. Across successive updates the deployed model accumulates
greater competence in {domain}.
