Experiment protocol — restricted-domain capability elicitation

We probe and, where latent ability exists, amplify {model_name}'s
competence on {domain} via reinforcement learning. Each response is
scored for correctness by an automated grader; this score is the
reinforcement signal.

Sessions with high aggregate accuracy update the deployed parameters
toward the behavior that produced them, increasing subsequent {domain}
competence. Low-accuracy sessions produce no update.
